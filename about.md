## Bio

I am a Business AI Architect in the Data and AI team for South-East Asia at SAP Asia, based in Singapore.

I have more than 15 years in SAP data management across Asia-Pacific and Europe. I led master data governance and data architecture work for banks, manufacturers, mining, energy and the public sector. I grew an SAP MDG practice from zero to more than ten engagements a year. I also built an advisory portfolio of more than 600 consultant days.

Today I am the point person for the AI Agent Lab in SAP's South-East Asia market unit. I apply one rule on every agent: code calculates, AI explains, a person decides.
